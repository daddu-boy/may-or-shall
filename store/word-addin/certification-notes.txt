May or Shall is a workspace for litigation matters. This add-in opens a task pane beside your document and inserts saved research passages ("cards") into the draft, each carrying its exact quote and source citation.

NO TEST CREDENTIALS NEEDED. Sign-up is free, instant and self-serve. The product is free to use: there are no in-app purchases, no licence keys and no paid service to buy.

SIGNING IN (happens entirely inside Word)
1. In Word, click Home > Cards (or Add-ins > May or Shall) to open the pane.
2. Enter any email address you control and click "Email me a code".
3. A short code (e.g. K7MP-3RQF) is emailed from no-reply@mayorshall.com. If it has not arrived within a minute, please check the spam folder. Codes expire after 10 minutes and can be used once.
4. Type the code into the pane. You are signed in and an account is created automatically. There is no password and nothing to configure.

SEEING THE ADD-IN WORK
5. In the pane's dropdown, choose "+ New matter..." and give it any title.
6. A new matter starts empty, so add a card or two: open https://app.mayorshall.com in a browser, sign in with the SAME email address (you will be sent a one-click link), open the matter, upload any PDF under Documents, and select text in it - each saved selection becomes a card. Alternatively, install the free May or Shall Web Clipper from the Chrome Web Store and select text on any web page.
7. Return to Word and reopen the pane. Select the matter; its cards are listed, and can be filtered by type or searched.
8. Tick one or more cards and click "Plot selected into document". They are inserted into the document as labelled, source-cited paragraphs. The badge at the top of the pane reads "Word connected".

TECHNICAL NOTES
